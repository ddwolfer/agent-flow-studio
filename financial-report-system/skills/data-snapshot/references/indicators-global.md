## Interpretation Guidelines

### Cross-Market Signals
- **Risk-On**: Equities ↑, VIX ↓, DXY ↓, Gold ↓, HY Spreads ↓, EM currencies ↑
- **Risk-Off**: Equities ↓, VIX ↑, DXY ↑, Gold ↑, Treasuries ↑, HY Spreads ↑
- **Reflation Trade**: Commodities ↑, Cyclicals > Defensives, Yield curve steepening
- **Deflation Fear**: Bonds ↑, Gold ↑, Commodities ↓, Yield curve flattening
- **Stagflation**: Commodities ↑, Equities ↓, Bonds ↓, Gold ↑

### Commodity Signals
- **Copper**: Leading economic indicator, rises with industrial demand
- **Oil**: Demand-driven rise = economic strength, supply-driven rise = cost-push inflation
- **Gold**: Rises with inflation expectations, real rate decline, and geopolitical risk
- **DXY**: Inverse to EM assets, commodity prices; strong dollar = EM headwind
