## Logical Fallacies in Financial Analysis

| Fallacy | Description | Financial Example | How to Detect |
|---------|------------|------------------|--------------|
| **Cherry-picking** | Selecting only supporting data | "過去 3 次降息台股都漲" (ignoring the other times) | Ask: What's the FULL sample? |
| **Post hoc ergo propter hoc** | X then Y, so X caused Y | "外資買超後大盤就漲" (might be reversed causality) | Ask: Could Y cause X? Is there a third factor? |
| **Appeal to authority** | Expert said it, so it must be true | "巴菲特說..." without checking if context applies | Ask: Is the expert's context the same as ours? |
| **Survivorship bias** | Only looking at winners | "這個策略過去 5 年都賺錢" (what about strategies that failed?) | Ask: What about the ones that didn't survive? |
| **Recency bias** | Overweighting recent events | "市場上週大跌所以衰退要來了" | Ask: Does one week make a trend? |
| **Anchoring** | Stuck on a reference number | "台積電合理價是 500" (based on old data) | Ask: Has anything changed since that estimate? |
| **Narrative fallacy** | Compelling story > data | "AI 革命所以科技股只漲不跌" | Ask: What does the DATA say vs the STORY? |
| **Composition fallacy** | Part true → whole true | "台積電好所以台股好" (ignores other 60% of index) | Ask: Is the part representative of the whole? |
| **False dichotomy** | Only two options presented | "要嘛軟著陸要嘛硬著陸" | Ask: Are there other possible outcomes? |
| **Slippery slope** | One event → extreme outcome | "升息一碼就會引發全面衰退" | Ask: What are the intermediate steps? Are they certain? |
| **Gambler's fallacy** | Past random events affect future | "已經連跌 5 天所以明天一定漲" | Ask: Is each event truly independent? |
| **Sunk cost fallacy** | Holding because already lost | "已經跌 30% 了不甘心賣" | Ask: Would you BUY at this price today? |
| **Availability bias** | Vivid events seem more likely | "2008 金融海嘯會重演" | Ask: What is the actual probability? |
| **Confirmation bias** | Seeking confirming evidence | Only reading bullish analysis when long | Ask: What would a smart bear say? |
| **Ad hominem** | Attacking the person, not the argument | "他又不是經濟學家，他的分析不可信" | Ask: Is the argument valid regardless of who says it? |
| **Strawman** | Misrepresenting the opposing view to attack it | "看空的人就是說台股會歸零" (nobody said that) | Ask: Is this a fair representation of the other side? |
| **Hasty generalization** | Drawing broad conclusions from small samples | "我認識的朋友都賺錢了所以這策略有效" | Ask: Is the sample representative? How large? |
| **Absolute vs relative risk** | Confusing percentage change with absolute magnitude | "風險增加 50%" (from 2% to 3%, not 50% absolute) | Ask: What are the BASE numbers? |
| **Misleading averages** | Using mean when median is more appropriate | "平均薪資成長 5%" (pulled up by top earners) | Ask: What does the MEDIAN show? Distribution shape? |
| **Dunning-Kruger** | Overconfidence in limited knowledge | "我看了 3 部影片就懂總經了" | Ask: What DON'T I know? |

## Cognitive Biases in Market Analysis

### Biases That Cause Over-Optimism
| Bias | Mechanism | Antidote |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Optimism bias | Things will work out | Assign specific failure probabilities |
| Overconfidence | I'm right, high conviction | Track prediction accuracy over time |
| Status quo bias | Current trend continues | Consider regime change scenarios |
| Bandwagon effect | Everyone's buying | Check contrarian indicators |

### Biases That Cause Over-Pessimism
| Bias | Mechanism | Antidote |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Loss aversion | Losses hurt 2x more than gains | Frame in terms of expected value, not potential loss |
| Negativity bias | Bad news weighs more | Equal time to positive developments |
| Zero-risk bias | Seeking certainty in uncertain world | Accept probabilistic thinking |
| Declinism | Things are getting worse | Compare objective data over time |

### Biases That Cause Bad Timing
| Bias | Mechanism | Antidote |
|------|-----------|---------|
| FOMO | Fear of missing out → buy high | Wait for your entry criteria, not market excitement |
| Panic selling | Fear → sell low | Pre-commit to stop-loss levels, not emotional reactions |
| Disposition effect | Sell winners too early, hold losers | Evaluate each position as if buying today |

## How to Use This in Cross-Check

### For Every Claim Being Verified:

1. **Read the claim**
2. **Scan this fallacy list** — does the claim contain any of these patterns?
3. **For each fallacy detected**:
   - Name it explicitly
   - Explain how it applies
   - Rate severity: Minor (doesn't invalidate the claim) / Moderate (weakens the claim) / Major (largely invalidates)
4. **Report in output table**:

| Fallacy | Present? | Severity | Explanation |
|---------|---------|---------|-------------|
| Cherry-picking | ✓/✗ | Minor/Moderate/Major | {how it manifests} |
| Confirmation bias | ✓/✗ | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

### Meta-Check: Am I Biased in My Verification?
- Am I looking harder for evidence that supports or contradicts?
- Would I reach the same conclusion if the claim came from a different source?
- Am I anchoring on the first piece of evidence I found?
