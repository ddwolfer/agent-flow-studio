## Sector Analysis Framework

1. **Relative Performance**: Each sector ETF vs SPY (S&P 500) — 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, YTD
2. **Valuation**: Forward P/E vs 5-year average
3. **Earnings Momentum**: EPS revision direction (30d, 90d)
4. **Technical**: RSI, 50d/200d MA relationship, relative strength line
5. **Fund Flows**: ETF inflow/outflow trends

## Rotation Signals

- **Defensive → Cyclical rotation**: Utilities/Staples underperforming while Industrials/Financials outperforming = improving economic outlook
- **Cyclical → Defensive rotation**: Reverse of above = deteriorating outlook
- **Growth → Value rotation**: XLK/XLC underperforming while XLF/XLE outperforming = rising rates/inflation regime
- **Value → Growth rotation**: Reverse = falling rates/disinflation regime
